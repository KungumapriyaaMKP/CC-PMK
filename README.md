## Architectural & Design Highlights
- **No Em Dashes & No Emojis**: Strictly compliant with clean typographical standards and bespoke SVG iconography.
- **Brand Geometry & Palette**: Crisp white background, deep navy (`#0A1128`, `#0C2F70`, `#081735`), brand cyan/sky blue (`#0284C7`, `#38BDF8`, `#4FA8D1`), and slate accents.
- **Interactive Metrics Counter**: Documented milestones (2 Crore+ campaign views, 11 Million single reel, 7 Million healthcare reel, 46,500+ niche community).
- **Comprehensive Case Studies**: Full-scope interactive modals for Eighteen Modesty (Fashion), Invest With Banu (Dubai Real Estate), Resilio The Pain Clinic (Healthcare), and Space By KV (Interior Design).
- **The 6-Year Analytics Differentiator**: Showcasing enterprise data analytics depth (SQL, Python, PySpark, ETL, Amazon Athena) applied to viral social media distribution.
- **Interactive Strategy Scope Calculator**: Lets prospective clients select their industry, audience stage, and goals to generate an instant strategic recommendation and pre-filled WhatsApp inquiry.
- **Direct WhatsApp & Inquiry Form**: One-click direct chat and consultation booking.
